# Tamil Nadu Cooking Methods Reference

Preparation method affects nutrition and allergen exposure, but dish names do not prove the method used by a particular kitchen. Read the central SQLite `cooking_method`/`preparation_method` fields when populated; otherwise say the restaurant method is not recorded.

- **Steaming:** common for idli, idiyappam, kozhukattai, and some vegetables. Batter composition, fillings, and accompaniments remain relevant.
- **Griddle cooking:** common for dosa, adai, pesarattu, and egg dosa. Oil/ghee and portion size vary.
- **Fermentation:** used for several rice/urad batters; fermentation time and ingredients vary.
- **Porridge cooking:** ragi kali and kambu koozh involve cooked millet and water; dilution, dairy, salt, and fermentation can vary.
- **Tempering/sautéing:** used for pongal, rice dishes, sundal, poriyal, and gravies. Oil/ghee and seasoning quantities are not known unless recorded.
- **Simmering:** used for sambar, rasam, kootu, kuzhambu, fish curry, and chicken curry. Salt, coconut, dairy, and oil vary.
- **Grilling/frying:** grilled chicken may have marinades and shared equipment. Fish fry can be pan- or deep-fried. Do not infer oil quantity or cross-contact from the food name.
