# Health Considerations for Tamil Menu Questions

NutriBot and NutriMatch provide general menu information only. They do not diagnose, treat, prescribe diets or medication, or override clinician advice. A traditional food is not automatically healthy or suitable for a particular person.

For high blood sugar, discuss only recorded carbohydrate and portion data; include recipe, grain, fibre, added sugar, sides, and meal context when known. Millet, ragi, rice, and traditional foods can all contribute carbohydrate. Individual response varies.

For high blood pressure/lower sodium, sodium must be measured and attached to the exact serving before calling a dish lower sodium. Salt and preparation vary. For low blood pressure, do not recommend salt or extra fluids as a default and do not claim a dish treats hypotension.

For heart health, total fat does not provide saturated/trans fat, and sodium may be missing. For kidney conditions, targets are highly individualized; recommend a qualified professional. For weight goals, present menu facts rather than diet prescriptions or guaranteed results.

Suitable response labels: Generally Suitable, Portion Considerations, Preparation Matters, Check Ingredients, Insufficient Information, Seek Professional Advice. Explain uncertainty and do not make absolute medical safety claims.
