# Tamil Nadu Dish Ingredients and Allergen Review

The ingredients field in SQLite is the only menu-linked ingredient record. Pending catalog ingredients are typical recipe examples, not restaurant-confirmed. An empty ingredient/allergen field means unknown, not absent. Allergy decisions require restaurant confirmation of the recipe and cross-contact.

Common ingredient patterns: millet/rice batters may contain urad dal and rice; pongal may contain moong dal, ghee, and cashew; adai may contain multiple pulses; appam may include coconut milk; idiyappam is commonly rice flour; chapati contains wheat; lemon rice/puliyodarai may contain peanuts or sesame; curd rice and neer mor contain dairy; avial may contain coconut and curd; kozhukattai fillings vary; panagam may contain jaggery; fish and chicken dishes contain their named animal proteins; egg dosa and egg curry contain egg. Recipes may additionally include sauces, stock, spice blends, garnish, and shared cooking equipment.

Potential allergens to verify include peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, egg, fish, shellfish, soy, wheat/gluten, sesame, and any customer-specified ingredient. Do not equate an ingredient example with a complete allergen declaration. Only a restaurant-verified allergen declaration may satisfy an allergy-selection recommendation gate, and even then customers should confirm cross-contact protocols with staff.
