# Weight and Protein Preference Guidance

Weight goals are optional preference signals, not diagnoses or prescriptions. NutriMatch may compare current listed energy or protein using a documented, fixed ranking formula. Missing values receive a neutral score and are never imputed. Do not calculate calorie targets from age, height, or weight in this implementation; optional personal measurements are not submitted or saved.

For weight-loss preferences, a lower listed-calorie item is only a menu comparison; no food guarantees weight loss. Portion size, overall pattern, preparation, fullness, and individual needs matter. Avoid shame or moralizing language.

For weight gain, muscle building, or high-protein preferences, compare listed calories/protein where available. Do not prescribe portions, supplements, or daily targets. A high menu protein value does not establish suitability for a person's medical needs. Suggest a qualified dietitian for individualized goals or unintentional weight change.
