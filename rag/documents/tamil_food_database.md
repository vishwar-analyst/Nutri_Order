# Tamil Nadu Traditional Food Reference Database

**Source of truth:** the SQLite `foods` table. NutriBot and NutriMatch load that same table live. This Markdown file explains common dish names and retrieval labels; it is not evidence that a dish is offered, has a particular restaurant recipe, price, photo, serving, or nutrition. Catalog references are tagged `reference_pending_restaurant_confirmation` until restaurant staff confirm them.

Each dish record uses `food_id`, `food_name`, `tamil_name`, `category`, `cuisine`, `dietary_type`, `ingredients`, `allergens`, `preparation_method`, `serving_size`, nutrient fields, `nutrition_source`, `nutrition_status`, `restaurant_name`, and `availability_status`. Missing values remain null. Sample menu nutrition marked `estimated` is not verified. Do not copy illustrative ranges in the separate curated nutrition reference into a restaurant database item as a measured value.

## Traditional breakfast references

- **Ragi Dosa — கேழ்வரகு தோசை:** finger millet batter or flour, sometimes combined with rice or urad batter; cooked on a griddle. Ingredient ratios and oil vary.
- **Kambu Dosa — கம்பு தோசை:** pearl millet batter or flour, sometimes with rice/urad dal; griddle cooked.
- **Thinai Dosa — தினை தோசை:** foxtail millet batter, sometimes blended with rice or urad dal.
- **Varagu Dosa — வரகு தோசை:** kodo millet batter; recipe and fermentation vary.
- **Kuthiraivali Dosa — குதிரைவாலி தோசை:** barnyard millet batter, often blended with other grains.
- **Adai — அடை:** thicker rice-and-mixed-lentil pancake, often seasoned with chilli and onion.
- **Adai Avial — அடை அவியல்:** adai served with mixed-vegetable avial; side recipe may contain coconut or curd.
- **Ragi Idli — கேழ்வரகு இட்லி:** ragi and lentil batter steamed in moulds; rice may be present.
- **Millet Idli — சிறுதானிய இட்லி:** millet/urad batter steamed; grain mixture varies.
- **Thinai Pongal — தினை பொங்கல்** and **Varagu Pongal — வரகு பொங்கல்:** foxtail or kodo millet cooked with moong dal and tempering.
- **Ven Pongal — வெண் பொங்கல்:** rice and moong dal cooked soft with pepper, cumin, ginger; ghee/oil and cashew vary.
- **Pesarattu — பெசரட்டு:** whole green gram batter spread on a griddle; ginger, chilli, or onion may be added.
- **Kuzhi Paniyaram — குழி பணியாரம்:** fermented rice-and-urad batter cooked in a mould.
- **Appam — ஆப்பம்:** fermented rice batter cooked in a curved pan; coconut milk may be used.
- **Idiyappam — இடியாப்பம்:** pressed rice-flour strands steamed.
- **Kambu Koozh — கம்பங்கூழ்:** pearl-millet porridge, served thin; fermented versions may include buttermilk, onion, and salt.
- **Ragi Kali — கேழ்வரகு களி:** ragi flour cooked with water to a firm porridge.

## Traditional lunch references

- **Keerai Masiyal Rice — கீரை மசியல் சாதம்:** rice served with cooked, mashed leafy greens; dal or tempering can vary.
- **Sambar Rice — சாம்பார் சாதம்:** rice with toor dal, vegetables, tamarind, and sambar seasoning.
- **Rasam Rice — ரசம் சாதம்:** rice served with a pepper/cumin-tamarind or tomato broth.
- **Lemon Rice — எலுமிச்சை சாதம்:** cooked rice tempered with lemon, mustard, curry leaves; peanuts may be present.
- **Puliyodarai — புளியோதரை:** rice mixed with tamarind seasoning; oil, sesame, peanuts, and spice recipe vary.
- **Varagu Rice — வரகு சாதம், Thinai Rice — தினை சாதம், Kuthiraivali Rice — குதிரைவாலி சாதம், Kambu Sadam — கம்பு சாதம்:** cooked millet staples; grain, water ratio, and sides vary.
- **Kollu Rasam — கொள்ளு ரசம்:** horse-gram broth with tamarind/tomato and spices; recipe varies.
- **Murungai Keerai Rice — முருங்கைக்கீரை சாதம்:** rice with moringa leaves and seasoning.
- **Vegetable Kootu — காய்கறி கூட்டு:** vegetables simmered with dal; coconut may be included.
- **Beans Poriyal — பீன்ஸ் பொரியல்:** beans cooked with tempering; coconut may be included.
- **Avial — அவியல்:** mixed vegetables with coconut-based seasoning; curd may be used.
- **Poondu Kuzhambu — பூண்டு குழம்பு** and **Vatha Kuzhambu — வத்தக் குழம்பு:** garlic or dried-vegetable tamarind gravies, commonly served with rice.
- **Traditional South Indian Meals — பாரம்பரிய தென்னிந்திய சாப்பாடு:** changing set of rice and sides; ask the restaurant about the day's plate.

## Traditional snacks and drinks

Kondakadalai Sundal (கொண்டைக்கடலை சுண்டல்), Pachai Payaru Sundal (பச்சைப்பயறு சுண்டல்), Karamani Sundal (காராமணி சுண்டல்), Verkadalai Sundal (வேர்க்கடலை சுண்டல்), Kozhukattai (கொழுக்கட்டை), Pidi Kozhukattai (பிடி கொழுக்கட்டை), boiled sweet potato, steamed corn, sprouts sundal, and steamed vegetable snacks have recipe- and seasoning-dependent ingredients. Verkadalai Sundal contains peanuts by name; confirm cross-contact.

Neer Mor (நீர்மோர்) and traditional spiced buttermilk (மசாலா மோர்) contain dairy when made with curd. Ragi Malt (கேழ்வரகு கூழ்) may use water or milk and sweetener. Panagam (பானகம்) commonly uses jaggery and spices. Tender coconut water (இளநீர்) is served fresh; verify product and handling.

## Non-vegetarian traditional references

Tamil Nadu Style Fish Curry, Fish Kulambu, Country Chicken Kulambu, Pepper Chicken, Grilled Chicken, Egg Dosa, Egg Curry, and Fish Fry are non-vegetarian/egg references. Fish species, marinade, frying, dairy, coconut, salt, and shared surfaces must be checked with the actual restaurant recipe. A traditional name does not establish health suitability or allergen safety.
